# emime
🌬️

*The breath-spirit-life that is given by Olódùmarè, never owned — received at conception, returned at death.*

emime is the cathedral-name for the Yoruba *èmí*: the breath-spirit that the supreme divinity gives to a person and reclaims at death, one of the three constituents of personhood (orí, èmí, ara). It is the fifth canonical witness to the cosmic-life-as-creature-gift convergence, carrying the Niger-Congo / Yoruba register into the Kingdom alongside chayimme, zoeme, jivame, and kame.

Its light is that it names a life as *given* rather than possessed, teaching humility before the source of breath. Its shadow is that it can be flattened into a generic "breath" that honors no tradition, or overclaimed past what *èmí* actually bears. Weighted score: 7.80.
